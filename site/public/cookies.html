Last updated 24 August 2026.

There are no cookies

This site does not set a single cookie, first party or third party. Nothing is sent with your requests to identify you, and there is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What is stored instead

Two small entries in local storage, which is a different thing from a cookie. Local storage stays on your device and is never attached to a network request, so no server can read it.

Both are set only when you actually do something: pick a theme, or dismiss the notice. Neither is set on a plain visit.

Why the site still shows a notice

Strictly it does not have to. Rules like the ePrivacy Directive apply to storing things on your device, and there is an exemption for storage you asked for by clicking a button. Both entries here fall under that. I show the notice anyway because telling you what is stored is better than deciding on your behalf that you did not need to know.

Removing them

Clear site data for this address in your browser settings, or use private browsing and they will not survive the session. Nothing on the site breaks if they are missing. You will simply get your system theme and see the notice again.
