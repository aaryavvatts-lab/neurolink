Privacy policy

Privacy

What this site collects, which is almost nothing, and what happens to anything you put into it.

Last updated 24 August 2026.

The short version

This site does not have accounts, does not use cookies, does not run analytics, and does not send anything you do here to anyone. Pictures you drop into the tools stay on your own device. There is no server doing any of the work.

Who runs this

A student project by Aaryav Sharma. It is a reanalysis of a public brain recording. It is not a company, not a product, and there is nothing to buy. You can reach me through the issues page on the code repository.

What is stored on your device

Two things, both in your browser and both under your control:

  • Your light or dark choice. Saved under the name neurolink.theme so the site looks the same next time.
  • Whether you dismissed the notice at the bottom. Saved as neurolink.cookieNotice so it does not keep reappearing.

Neither is a cookie. Both use local storage, which means they never travel with a request and no server ever sees them. Clearing your browser data for this site removes both.

What happens to a picture you upload

Nothing leaves your device. When you drop a picture into the response tool, your browser draws it onto a hidden canvas, measures it, and multiplies those measurements by a small set of numbers it downloaded with the page. The picture is never sent anywhere and is never written to storage. Close the tab and it is gone.

You can check this yourself. Open your browser developer tools, go to the network tab, and drop a picture in. You will see no upload.

Server logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, their servers record ordinary request information when a page is fetched: the address requested, an IP address, a timestamp, and the browser user agent string. I have not enabled Vercel Analytics or any other product that builds a profile from that, and I have no dashboard showing me who visited. What Vercel keeps and for how long is covered by their privacy policy.

Other sites

This site loads no fonts, scripts, images or trackers from anywhere else. Everything it needs is served from the same address, including the 3D library. Links out to papers and to GitHub are ordinary links, and following one takes you to a site with its own policy.

Children

There is nothing here aimed at children and nothing collected from anyone, so there is no age-related data handling to describe.

Your rights

Rules like the GDPR and the UK GDPR give you rights over personal data held about you, including asking to see it or have it deleted. I do not hold any personal data about you, so there is nothing for me to show or delete. If you want the two local storage entries gone, clearing site data in your browser does it immediately and does not need my involvement.

Patient data

The brain recordings shown here came from two people who agreed to have their data shared publicly. It was released under a licence that puts it in the public domain. Nothing on this site identifies them. The brain surfaces in the 3D viewer have had the facial features removed by the people who released the data, which is standard practice so a scan cannot be matched back to a face.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the old version stays in the git history of the repository where anyone can read it.
