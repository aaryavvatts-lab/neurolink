What this is built on

Everything below is a real paper with a working link. I have read the parts I lean on and said in the text where each one is used.

The data

  • Hermes D, Miller KJ, Wandell BA, Winawer J (2025). iEEG_visual, OpenNeuro dataset ds005953, version 1.0.0. Released CC0. doi:10.18112/openneuro.ds005953.v1.0.0

Where the gamma story comes from

  • Hermes D, Miller KJ, Wandell BA, Winawer J (2015). Stimulus dependence of gamma oscillations in human visual cortex. Cerebral Cortex 25(9), 2951–2959. doi:10.1093/cercor/bhu091
    The paper behind this dataset. It is the source of the result I use as a gate: stripes set off a narrow gamma bump, grainy textures do not.
  • Hermes D, Petridou N, Kay KN, Winawer J (2019). An image-computable model for the stimulus selectivity of gamma oscillations. doi:10.1101/583567
    Where the two-part spectrum model comes from: a straight line plus a bump, with the slope fixed per contact from its own baseline.
  • Ray S, Maunsell JHR (2011). Different origins of gamma rhythm and high-gamma activity in macaque visual cortex. PLoS Biology 9(4), e1000610. doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.1000610
    The reason the two parts have to be separated rather than summed. They come from different things.
  • Shirhatti V, Ravishankar P, Ray S (2022). Gamma oscillations in primate primary visual cortex are severely attenuated by small stimulus discontinuities. PLoS Biology 20(6), e3001666. doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.3001666
  • Brunet NM, Fries P (2019). Human visual cortical gamma reflects natural image structure. NeuroImage 200, 635–643. doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2019.06.051

What these recordings are and how well they decode

  • Buzsáki G, Anastassiou CA, Koch C (2012). The origin of extracellular fields and currents: EEG, ECoG, LFP and spikes. Nature Reviews Neuroscience 13, 407–420. doi:10.1038/nrn3241
  • Kanth ST, Ray S (2020). Electrocorticogram (ECoG) is highly informative in primate visual cortex. Journal of Neuroscience 40(12), 2430–2444. doi:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1368-19.2020
    Useful for setting expectations: this kind of recording decodes visual stimuli better than several finer-grained alternatives.
  • Dubey A, Ray S (2019). Cortical electrocorticogram (ECoG) is a local signal. Journal of Neuroscience 39(22), 4299–4311. doi:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2917-18.2019

The models I used or copied the idea from

  • Oquab M, Darcet T, Moutakanni T, and colleagues (2023). DINOv2: learning robust visual features without supervision. arXiv:2304.07193
    The image model whose space I map the brain signals into.
  • Wang C, Subramaniam V, Yaari A, and colleagues (2023). BrainBERT: self-supervised representation learning for intracranial recordings. ICLR. arXiv:2302.14367
    The recipe I followed for the model I trained myself: blank out patches of a spectrogram and learn to fill them in.
  • Jiang W-B, Zhao L-M, Lu B-L (2024). Large Brain Model for learning generic representations with tremendous EEG data in BCI. arXiv:2405.18765
    One of the scalp EEG foundation models. Included for context on what that family is trained on.
  • Ye J, Collinger JL, Wehbe L, Gaunt R (2023). Neural Data Transformer 2: multi-context pretraining for neural spiking activity. doi:10.1101/2023.09.18.558113
