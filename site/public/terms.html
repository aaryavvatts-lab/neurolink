What this is

A student research project that presents a reanalysis of a public brain recording, plus two tools that run in your browser. Using the site means you accept what is written here.

This is not medical advice

Nothing on this site is medical advice, a diagnosis, or a medical device. It is not approved by any regulator and has never been tested for clinical use. The recordings come from two patients having epilepsy surgery, and results from two people do not tell you anything about yourself or anyone else. If you have a health question, ask a doctor.

What the tools do and do not promise

The response tool predicts what visual cortex might do given a picture. It was fitted on 210 trials from one person who was looking at stripes and grainy textures. Its accuracy is printed next to every prediction, and it warns you when your picture sits outside what it learned. Treat its output as a rough guide for planning, not as a measurement.

The decoder tool replays a real model on real trials. It shows what that model produced. It does not read minds, it cannot be pointed at a living person, and it only works on the specific recordings shipped with the page.
